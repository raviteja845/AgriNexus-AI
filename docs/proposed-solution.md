# Proposed Solution

AgriNexus AI is planned as an agricultural market decision-support platform.

A farmer enters crop type, location, quantity, and expected harvest date. The system retrieves available market and weather information, analyzes possible selling locations, estimates transport costs, and presents comparable net-return scenarios.

## Recommendation format

Each recommendation should show:
- Market and price data timestamp
- Reported price and unit
- Estimated transport cost and assumptions
- Estimated net return
- Data gaps and forecast uncertainty
- Reasons supporting the recommendation

The farmer remains in control of the final selling decision.
