# Problem Statement

## Farmers sell at the wrong time, place, and price

Farmers may not have easy access to timely and comparable market information. Price changes, demand differences between markets, weather risks, and transport expenses complicate selling decisions.

## Questions the project aims to address

- What prices are reported in relevant nearby markets?
- How do current prices compare with recent historical patterns?
- What are the estimated transport costs?
- Which market may provide the strongest estimated net return?
- What uncertainty or missing information should the farmer consider?

The project aims to support decisions with transparent data rather than promise a particular selling price.
